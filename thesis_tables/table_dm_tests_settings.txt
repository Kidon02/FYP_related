Dataset: S&P 500, Nikkei 225, FTSE 100 test predictions (results/test_predictions_{IDX}.csv)
Feature set: domestic_only vs cross_market (lag 1), domestic_only vs cross_market_lag0, cross_market vs cross_market_lag0
Label strategy: baseline, hmm, regime_std
Model: xgboost and logistic_regression (seed-mean probabilities)
Seed: 42-46 (5 seeds; mean and std over seeds), HMM random_state 42
Config file: configs/dm_tests.yaml
Checkpoint: not saved (models are refit inside code/baseline_models.py)
Test window: 2019-01-01 to 2024-12-31
Notes: loss = squared error (daily Brier score) of the seed-mean probability after undoing SMOTE's class balance with the 2000-2015 training prevalence (monotone; PR-AUC unchanged); d = loss_a - loss_b. Newey-West long-run variance (Bartlett, lags floor(4(n/100)^(2/9)) = 7), Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold factor for h=1, Student t(n-1), two-sided p-values; no multiple-comparison correction. After the correction, mean probabilities are 0.4-1.3x the test prevalence for logistic regression but 0.05-0.8x for XGBoost, so XGBoost Brier differences partly reflect calibration. Raw-probability tests: results/dm_tests.csv (*_raw).
